WHO WE ARE

A basketball community built in Gaya.

Established in 2008 with a small group of around 20 players, GDBA has grown into a district-wide basketball community working with schools and clubs to create pathways for young players.

Our work focuses on grassroots participation, structured training, district competitions, talent identification and opportunities to represent Gaya at state and national levels.

OUR PURPOSE01

Make Gaya a strong basketball hub.

We aim to develop talented players, strengthen schools and clubs, support coaches and officials, and create a positive sporting environment for boys and girls.
